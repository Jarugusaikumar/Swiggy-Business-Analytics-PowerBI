# Business Insights

## Dataset-based observations
The following insight examples are grounded in the synthetic data included in this repository.

### 1. Revenue is concentrated in a few major cities
- Business observation: Revenue is highly concentrated in a small number of cities.
- Supporting KPI: Mumbai generated approximately ₹92.16M in revenue, followed by Delhi at ₹75.03M and Bangalore at ₹62.30M.
- Possible business reason: Dense urban demand, larger customer base, and higher order frequency drive higher order value in metro markets.
- Recommended action: Prioritize local marketing, restaurant expansion, and city-level operations support in these cities while evaluating secondary cities for growth.

### 2. Cancellation rate remains manageable but should be monitored
- Business observation: The dataset has a cancellation rate of 4.95% across 600,000 orders.
- Supporting KPI: 29,719 cancelled orders out of 600,000 total orders.
- Possible business reason: Stock-outs, menu unavailability, and delivery delays are common causes in food delivery operations.
- Recommended action: Improve inventory visibility, restaurant response time, and order acceptance quality to reduce avoidable cancellations.

### 3. Average order value is strong and supports premium upsell opportunities
- Business observation: The dataset shows a healthy average order value of ₹694.93.
- Supporting KPI: Total revenue = ₹416,956,363.49 across 600,000 orders.
- Possible business reason: Basket size is strong across multiple cuisine and menu categories, which supports cross-sell and combo-based promotions.
- Recommended action: Bundle promotions, chef specials, and regional meal offers can convert average-order-value gains into higher revenue without sacrificing retention.

### 4. Customer behavior is skewed toward a minority of highly active users
- Business observation: Order frequency varies significantly across customers.
- Supporting KPI: Mean orders per customer = 60; median = 13; top customer revenue exceeds ₹2.27L.
- Possible business reason: A small subset of customers is highly loyal and contributes a disproportionate share of revenue.
- Recommended action: Use loyalty campaigns, personalized recommendations, and retention offers to turn repeat users into high-value advocates.

## Recommended dashboard narrative
The Power BI presentation should tell a story of growth, city concentration, keep-the-healthy-revenue mix, and operational controllability.

## Caution
These insights are based on synthetic data and should be interpreted as portfolio examples, not as live business performance claims.
